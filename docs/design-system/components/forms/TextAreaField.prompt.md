Two multi-line fields in one component.

```jsx
<TextAreaField variant="bare" rows={10} placeholder="What's your hoojah?" />
<TextAreaField label="Headline" rows={3} placeholder="Add some jazz to your profile!" />
<TextAreaField rows={3} placeholder="Make your argument…" />
```

The bare composer has no border or ring at all — the 18px text just sits beside your 44px avatar. The boxed variant borders light-grey and turns indigo on focus.
